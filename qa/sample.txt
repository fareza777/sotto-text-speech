The Lantern Keeper

Chapter One: The Long Winter

The house had been empty for eleven years, and yet the lamp in the upstairs window still burned every night. Mira had noticed it first from the road, a small orange seed against the blue dark. She had assumed it was a reflection, or a trick of the glass.

It was neither.

Chapter Two: A Room of Paper

Inside, the rooms smelled of cedar and old rain. Books climbed the walls in uneven towers. On the desk lay a ledger filled with careful handwriting, each entry a date and a name and a single sentence describing the weather.

The last entry was dated the night her brother disappeared.

Chapter Three: What the Light Knows

She read until the candle guttered. Somewhere below, the front door opened without a sound. The lamp above her brightened, just once, as if recognising a guest.

Mira did not turn around. She already knew who it was.
